# GTM Scorecard — Merchant Qualification (DRAFT)

**Purpose:** Score every lead BEFORE a sales call. Only pursue merchants who pass the economics test. A pretty logo is not a wedge; a recoverable dormant segment with clean data is.

**Wedge order (internal, never public):** Aesthetic clinics / med spas → premium salons & wellness → florists → cafés. Clinics have ticket size, repeat cycles, booked-consult funnels, dormant lead pools, and clean CRM/POS data. Cafés make great social proof, weak first-unit economics.

---

## Scoring (1–5 each, score ≥ 32/40 = pursue)

| # | Criterion | What good looks like | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Average order value | ≥ $60 (clinics/salons) or clear package/membership revenue | 5 |
| 2 | Repeat purchase / rebooking cycle | Natural rebooking interval ≤ 90 days, observable in data | 5 |
| 3 | Customer / lead records | ≥ 2,000 identifiable historical customers or leads | 5 |
| 4 | Data access | POS, CRM, booking, or payment-system access (read-only at first) | 5 |
| 5 | Contactable / consented audience | Valid phone/email with documented consent state; opt-out tooling exists | 5 |
| 6 | Owner/GM responsiveness | Can act on insights within 24–48 hours (tested in the first call) | 5 |
| 7 | Gross margin to share upside | Unit economics survive 10–15% of incremental revenue + operating cost | 5 |
| 8 | Dormant / recoverable segment | Visible lapsed customers or unbooked/never-followed-up leads | 5 |

## Red flags (auto-decline or gate behind data-readiness phase)

- < 500 usable customer records, or duplicate/consentless data
- No centralized payments (all-cash, no POS export)
- Owner unavailable / no single decision-maker
- AOV so low that 15% of incremental won't cover agent + support cost
- Merchant expects Socio to "just run ads" with no data access

## Data-readiness gate

Any merchant scoring 24–31 gets a **paid-by-success data-readiness phase** (30 days): consent cleanup, profile dedupe, POS mapping, baseline setup. Minimum data-quality thresholds must be met before campaign launch — else the merchant churns and Socio eats the labor.

## Post-sign measurement

Monthly Verified Growth Statement per `templates/revenue-definition-schedule.md` §9. Churn signal: if the number can't be explained in 60 seconds, the statement is failing, not the merchant.
