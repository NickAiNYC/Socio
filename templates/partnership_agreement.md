# SOCIO PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

**Between:** Socio LLC ("Socio")
[Merchant Name] ("Merchant")

**Date:** [Date]

**1. Partnership Structure**
Socio agrees to provide digital marketing, local SEO, and revenue growth services to Merchant. Socio is a performance-based partner, not an agency.

**2. Compensation**
- **Zero upfront fees.** Socio charges no setup fees, no retainers, no hidden charges.
- **Commission:** [10-15]% of Net New Revenue generated by Socio's efforts.
- **Net New Revenue defined as:** Revenue from customers who have not transacted with Merchant in the previous 12 months.
- **Payment terms:** Monthly, net 15 days.

**3. Term & Termination**
- **Initial term:** 12 months.
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.
- **No penalties, no exit fees.** (Compliance with NYC Click-to-Cancel: termination is as easy as signing up.)

**4. Data Ownership**
- Merchant owns all customer data.
- Socio only tracks commissionable revenue.
- Socio does not sell, share, or monetize Merchant data.

**5. AI Disclosure**
- Socio uses AI tools to assist with content creation.
- All AI-generated content will be labeled "AI-assisted" where required by law.
- No content is published without Merchant review and approval. (Compliance with WeChat's "non-human automation" principle: AI assists, humans approve.)

**6. All-In Pricing**
- The price Merchant pays is the commission rate only.
- No hidden fees, no junk fees, no surprise charges. (Compliance with NYC Junk Fee rule.)

**7. Liability**
- Socio carries $1M general liability insurance.
- Socio is not liable for Merchant's business operations, inventory, or employee matters.

**8. Governing Law**
- This agreement is governed by the laws of New York State.
