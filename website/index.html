FAQ

Questions,
answered honestly

How does the 12/8/5% commission work?

We only earn on Net New Revenue and Expansion Revenue — every dollar POS-matched to your register. Zero upfront fees, zero retainers. If we don't generate growth, we don't get paid.

How do you prove the revenue is real?

Square, Stripe or register receipts. Every claim traces through our Revenue Ledger — a tamper-evident audit chain. No vanity metrics, no hand-waving. If we can't trace it, we don't claim it.

What if it doesn't work?

Then we don't bill. Our model only pays on results. Your free Revenue Recovery Map comes first — delivered within 48 hours, before you commit to anything.

What do I have to do?

Run your business. We handle content calendars, directory listings, review management and campaigns. Every AI-generated asset requires your approval before it goes out.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, no penalties. Click-to-cancel. You own all your data, forever.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Read-only POS access for revenue matching only, encrypted in transit, $1M liability coverage, and we never share or sell your data.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí, hablamos español. Our outreach, content and reporting are fully bilingual (EN/ES).

Do I need to pay for ads?

No. We start with recovery campaigns on channels you already own — SMS/WhatsApp, email, listings, content. Paid media is always an approved, budgeted decision.
