FAQ

Questions,
answered honestly

How does the 10–15% commission work?

We only earn on Net New Revenue (brand-new customers) and Expansion Revenue (existing customers coming back more often) — every dollar POS-matched to your register. Zero upfront fees, zero retainers. If we don't generate growth, we don't get paid.

How do you prove the revenue is real?

Square, Stripe or register receipts. Every claim traces through our Revenue Ledger — a tamper-evident audit chain — and your monthly report shows exactly which transactions we attribute and how. No vanity metrics, no hand-waving. If we can't trace it, we don't claim it.

What if it doesn't work?

Then we don't bill. Our model only pays on results. Your free Revenue Recovery Map comes first — delivered within 48 hours, before you commit to anything. Once you onboard, our fleet starts executing immediately, with first campaigns typically live within the first week.

What do I have to do?

Run your business. We handle content calendars, directory listings, review management and campaigns. Every AI-generated asset is labeled and requires your approval before it goes out. You stay in control at all times.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, no penalties. Click-to-cancel — leaving is as easy as joining. You own all your data, forever.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Read-only POS access for revenue matching only, encrypted in transit, $1M liability coverage, and we never share or sell your data. AI Disclosure: everything generated by AI is clearly labeled.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí, hablamos español. Our outreach, content and reporting are fully bilingual (EN/ES) — because your customers are too.

Do I need to pay for ads?

No. We start with recovery campaigns on channels you already own — SMS/WhatsApp, email, listings, content. If paid media makes sense later, it's an approved, budgeted decision you sign off on. Never a requirement.

What can you — and can't you — claim? (Evidence vs Assumption)

We believe in radical honesty. Here is our exact boundary between verified mathematical evidence and speculative assumptions:

✓ What We Claim (Deterministic Evidence) ✗ What We Do NOT Claim (Speculative)
Exact POS & bank deposit timestamp match Causality without holdout baseline comparison
QuickBooks Online & Stripe cleared transaction hash Unverifiable "foot traffic" or "brand impression" lifts
New & reactivated customers outside historical baseline Organic word-of-mouth sales already happening
