FAQ

Questions contractors actually ask

What happens if the deal falls through after the walkthrough?

If the client does not deposit the project funds, Socio does not charge a commission. The current contractor agreement states that commission is generated only when the client deposits the project advance into the contractor's verified commercial account.

How do you actually find these leads?

Socio monitors filed NYC DOB NOW and BIS permit activity and uses those signals alongside registered lead and follow-up activity to identify renovation opportunities.

Is this compliant with DOB rules?

Socio uses publicly filed permit information as an opportunity signal. This page is not legal advice; contractors remain responsible for their own licensing, permitting, solicitation, and other legal obligations.

What if I don't want a specific lead?

You control whether to pursue an opportunity. The Socio model is performance-based, so a lead that does not become verified cleared revenue does not generate a commission.
