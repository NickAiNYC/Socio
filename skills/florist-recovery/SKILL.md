# Florist Recovery Skill

## Step 1 — Segment dormant customers
- Last purchase > 90 days ago
- Minimum lifetime spend > $50
- Not on suppression list (opted out, disputed, or charged back)

## Step 2 — Offer selection
- Holiday/event reminders (Mother's Day, Valentine's, Sympathy season)
- Repeat gifting (subscription bouquets, standing orders)
- Lapsed customer reactivation (20% off first order back)

## Step 3 — Channel selection
- WhatsApp for high-value customers (lifetime > $300)
- SMS for mid-value ($100–$300)
- Email for broad campaigns (lowest cost, highest volume)

## Step 4 — Attribution window
- 14 days for holiday campaigns
- 7 days for reactivation offers
- Record campaign/experiment id on every offer so revenue is attributable

## Rules
- Never send more than 2 messages per week per customer
- Always include an opt-out link
- Never discount below 20% without Governor approval
- Gift-card purchases never count as incremental revenue (see Revenue
  Definition Schedule exclusions)
- Log every offer as a governed action with an executed approval before send
