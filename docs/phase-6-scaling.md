# Phase 6: Scaling Strategy

## 1 → 50 Merchant Playbook

### Month 1: The First 3 (Validation)
- **Goal:** Sign 3 merchants (e.g., 1 Florist, 1 Restaurant, 1 Bodega).
- **Execution:** Heavy reliance on human-in-the-loop for pitch approvals and onboarding packets. Socio OS handles 70% of the workflow.

### Month 3: Reaching 15 (Standardization)
- **Goal:** Standardize the "141 SOPs" based on the first cohort.
- **Execution:** Shift from manual content review to batch approvals. DSH handles 100% of webhook processing and Synup listings. Automation climbs to 85%.
- **Revenue Target:** ~$5,000/month recurring commissions.

### Month 6: Scaling to 50 (Domination)
- **Goal:** Launch the NYC OPC Community and scale referral loops.
- **Execution:** 95% automation. The 8 operational agents manage all day-to-day work. The human founder transitions purely to a relationship manager and strategy reviewer. 
- **Revenue Target:** ~$20,000/month recurring commissions.
