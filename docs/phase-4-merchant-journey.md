# Phase 4: The Merchant Journey

## The 10-Stage Workflow

1. **PROSPECT (Socio-Prospect)**: Daily scraping of NYC neighborhoods, auditing digital gaps, and scoring top 10 targets.
2. **PITCH (Socio-Pitch)**: 3-touch bilingual email/WhatsApp sequence rooted in the Socio Manifesto.
3. **ONBOARD (Socio-Onboard)**: Generating partnership agreements, collecting data, and delivering a 48-hour audit.
4. **EXECUTE (Socio-Content & Socio-Listings)**: Deploying 30-day content calendars, maintaining local SEO, and automating review responses.
5. **TRACK (Socio-Track)**: Real-time calculation of Net New Revenue and Expansion Revenue from POS webhooks. Generating invoices.
6. **REVIEW (Socio-Content)**: Monthly performance reports and strategy review calls.
7. **REPORT (Socio-Track)**: Weekly dashboards summarizing commission and merchant growth.
8. **EXPAND (Socio-Expand)**: Identifying cross-sell opportunities (e.g. catering for restaurants) and driving referrals.
9. **REFERRAL (Socio-Expand)**: Activating a 5% commission-for-life referral incentive program.
10. **RENEW (Socio-Track)**: Annual ROI analysis and partnership renewal negotiation.
