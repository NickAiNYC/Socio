## 1. The 7 Layers Breakdown

### Layer 1: The Language (Payment-Cycle First)
* **Rule**: Speak to the merchant's cash flow, not your technical services.
* **Bad**: *"We generate leads and run AI marketing campaigns."*
* **Good**: *"No le cobramos nada hasta que el dinero del anticipo esté en su cuenta bancaria."*

### Layer 2: The Proof (Money in the Bank vs. Promises)
* **Rule**: Replace vanity metrics (*clicks, impressions, unverified leads*) with verified cleared dollars.
* **Doctrine**: Lead = a promise. Deposit = money in the bank.

### Layer 3: The Moat (Proprietary Data Ingestion)
* **Rule**: Ingest real-time public or industry feeds that generic agencies cannot touch.
* **Examples**:
  * Construction: NYC DOB NOW / BIS Permits Feed.
  * Floristas: Flower District wholesale supply & event filings.
  * Restaurantes: Neighborhood private dining & catering demand signals.
  * Clínicas: No-show prediction & treatment slot re-allocations.

### Layer 4: The Attribution (Deterministic Revenue-Event Trigger)
* **Rule**: Invoices are never dispatched until webhook confirmation from QuickBooks Online, Stripe, Clover, Square, or Toast verifies cleared funds.

### Layer 5: The Conversion (Brutally Honest Leak Scan)
* **Rule**: Don't offer a generic demo. Quantify what they are actively losing every 30 days due to missed calls and sluggish follow-up.

### Layer 6: The Trust (The Guaranteed Annual Cap)
* **Rule**: Remove fear of unlimited agency milking. Once the partner pays the vertical annual cap, commission drops to 0.0%.

### Layer 7: Vertical-Specific Psychology
* **Rule**: Never build a generic "Small Business" landing page. Every industry has distinct fears, language, and trade nodes.

## 3. Deployment Checklist for New Verticals

1. **Root Vertical Hub** (`/<vertical>`): High-converting light industrial / clean editorial UI with interactive calculator.
2. **Detailed Process** (`/<vertical>/como-funciona`): 4-step workflow explaining the revenue-cleared billing trigger.
3. **Proof & Case Studies** (`/<vertical>/casos-de-exito`): 3–6 real trade breakdowns with Before vs After metrics.
4. **Proprietary Moat Demo** (`/<vertical>/<tech-moat>`): Live visual simulation of proprietary data feed ingestion.
5. **Objection Killer FAQ** (`/<vertical>/preguntas-frecuentes`): Direct answers resolving agency PTSD, client cancellations, and annual cap protections.
6. **Supplier & Ecosystem Nodes** (`/proveedores/...`): Partner landing pages with 1% referral kickbacks.
7. **Sticky WhatsApp FAB (`#waFab`)**: Pre-filled conversational prompt customized to the visitor's vertical context.
