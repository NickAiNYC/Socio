# Phase 1: Vision & Core Identity

## The Socio DNA
Socio is a performance-based growth partnership firm for local NYC merchants, initially targeting florists, expanding later to restaurants, bodegas, and laundromats. 

**The Promise:** "We don't get paid until you do." Zero upfront. 10–15% commission on new revenue we generate.

**The Positioning:** We are not an agency. We are growth partners — "Socio" means partner in Spanish. We succeed only when our merchants succeed.

**The Metric:** Two numbers only — Net New Revenue and Expansion Revenue from existing partners.

**The Philosophy:** 10 great partners beat 100 mediocre ones. We go deep, not wide.

## Target Audience
Local NYC businesses (East Harlem, Washington Heights, Bronx, Queens) that are fundamentally strong offline but lack the digital infrastructure or bandwidth to maximize their online presence, reviews, and repeat customer marketing.
