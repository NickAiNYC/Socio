## Before you feed these to CREATOR

**On "FUGA" as the keyword:** in P1 I argued against opening a cold WhatsApp with "Escaneo de Fugas" — as a cold message it's jargon he has to decode. **On Instagram it works, for the opposite reason.** A keyword that means nothing yet *is* the curiosity gap, and he's already chosen to watch. Keep FUGA here. Just don't use it in Touch 1.

**On register:** these use `tú` more than the WhatsApp scripts do. A Reel is broadcast, not a personal address — `usted` in a video reads as stiff and old-fashioned, and the platform's native voice is casual. Keep `usted` in DMs, use `tú` on camera. That inconsistency is correct, not sloppy.

**Three rules for CREATOR:**

1. **The hook is the first 3 seconds of *spoken* audio AND on-screen text.** Both, simultaneously. Most people watch muted.
2. **Never open with the logo, the name, or "hola, soy Nick."** The hook is sentence one.
3. **Claims must be true.** Several hooks below use numbers — every one is either from a cited source or framed as a question rather than an assertion. Don't let CREATOR invent statistics to fill the format; that's the exact thing that will get this account distrusted in a neighborhood that talks.

## Production notes for CREATOR

**Format:** 15–25 seconds. Longer than 30 and the completion rate collapses.

**Structure every Reel identically:**

| Seconds | What |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hook — spoken *and* on-screen text, simultaneously |
| 3–15 | One idea. **One.** Not three. |
| 15–20 | The CTA, on screen and spoken |

**Captions:** burned in, always. Spanish. This audience watches muted on a jobsite.

**Face on camera:** Nick, for Blocks D and E especially. The anti-agency message cannot come from a faceless account — it's a trust message, and trust needs a face. Blocks A, B, and C can be B-roll with voiceover.

**Posting cadence:** 4–5 per week beats 30 in a burst. Sequence them: Block A → B → D → C → E, then repeat with the second half.

**What to actually measure:** not views. **DMs containing "FUGA."** A Reel with 400 views and 6 keyword DMs beats one with 20,000 views and zero, every time. Track hook-to-DM by block so you learn which *angle* works, not which video got lucky.

**Auto-reply on the FUGA keyword** — set it up before you post the first one. A keyword CTA with no responder is worse than no CTA. First message back should be short and human, and it should ask a question, not deliver a pitch.
