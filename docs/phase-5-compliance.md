# The 7-Day Compliance Sprint

This checklist is designed to be imported into Taskade Genesis to track your progress toward a fully compliant, bulletproof setup.

- [ ] **Day 1: Entity Setup**
  - Register Socio LLC (NYC)
  - Get EIN from IRS
- [ ] **Day 2: License & Protection**
  - Get NYC Business License
  - Purchase $1M general liability insurance
- [ ] **Day 3: Agreement Generation**
  - Draft partnership agreement (bilingual) via DSH
  - Create "AI Disclosure" policy for content
  - Create "All-In Pricing" disclosure template
- [ ] **Day 4: Technical & Legal Review**
  - Review partnership agreement and ToS with a licensed New York Lawyer
  - Set up Stripe Connect with commission tracking
  - Create merchant data privacy policy
- [ ] **Day 5: Verification & Publish**
  - Final legal review
  - Test click-to-cancel interface (if offering subscriptions)
  - Publish all policies on `socio.nyc`
