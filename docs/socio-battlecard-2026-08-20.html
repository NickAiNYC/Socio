Quick Win / Loss Guide

You win when — the prospect has been burned by an upfront-fee agency or a shared-lead marketplace before, speaks Spanish first, is a solo-to-small crew ($10K–$50K jobs), and values zero risk over a polished dashboard.
You lose when — the prospect wants a $150K+ project matched today (BuildZoom's territory), already runs Housecall Pro/ServiceTitan and just wants a bolt-on AI receptionist, or needs proof (case studies) you don't have yet since no pilot has closed.

Key Differentiators

  • Zero upfront, ever. Commission triggers only on a bank-cleared deposit in QuickBooks — the one claim every competitor in this set fails to make simultaneously with results-based pricing.
  • WhatsApp-native, not app- or portal-based. Meets contractors where they already work; no login, no dashboard to learn.
  • NYC hyperlocal data engine. DOB/HPD/LPC filings feed outbound targeting directly — nobody else in this set routes public permit data to contractor lead gen (DOBGuard sells the same category of data, but to property owners, not contractors).
  • Physical trust channel. Supply-house pro-desk relationships are slow to copy and don't exist in any competitor's stack.
  • Refuses to overclaim. The Economic Truth Model hard-codes "unknown attribution" rather than guessing — a governance discipline no competitor publishes.

Proof Points

Honest gap: zero verified deposits and zero case studies exist yet — Gate 1 (5 pilots signed) and Gate 2 (3 verified deposits) haven't been hit. Until then, the sellable proof is structural (the zero-upfront mechanism itself, and the kill-gate discipline), not testimonial. Do not claim results in the field that the ledger can't back — that discipline is itself the pitch.
